What this is

An experimental stock screener built by Valencia AI for testing and learning. It matches daily price data against a written set of rules and shows you the result. It has not been backtested, and nothing here is a prediction.

Not financial advice. Valencia AI is not a licensed advisor, broker or dealer. Nothing on this page is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. Any trade you make is your own decision and your own risk. Do your own research and consider talking to a licensed professional.

Data. The page opens with generated demo prices. For real quotes, sign up for a free API key at twelvedata.com, paste it below and click Run screen. Your key is stored only in this browser and requests go directly from you to Twelve Data. Quotes are end-of-day daily bars, delayed, for personal use only. How to use it (5 minutes)

Set up once

  1. Go to twelvedata.com, sign up (free), confirm your email.
  2. Log in, open the Dashboard, copy your API key.
  3. Paste it in the "Twelve Data API key" box below.
  4. Tick "Fetch earnings dates", click Run screen. It takes about 4 minutes and pauses between batches; that is normal, don't click again. The orange DEMO badge turns green when real data is in.

Every time you look

  1. Click Run screen to refresh. Best after 4pm Eastern, when the day's bar is final.
  2. Read the cards first. Breakout: closed above its prior 20-day high today (confirmed) or sitting just under it (watch). Pullback watch: healthy uptrend that dipped back to its average. Warnings: extended or heavy selling, read before anything else. Earnings within 7 days: avoid, it's a coin flip.
  3. Click a ticker name to open its chart and notes. The top note gives the verdict and why.

Before you buy anything

  1. Is the data current and the setup confirmed? Green LIVE badge, bar date is the last trading day. "No setup", "Downtrend" or a "watch" label that hasn't triggered means wait.
  2. Earnings and news? More than 7 days from earnings, and you've read today's news on it.
  3. What price proves you wrong? Pick the stop from the chart (below the pullback low, below the breakout level). The notes show the ATR so you can check the distance is reasonable. A stop order does not guarantee that price; gaps happen.
  4. How many shares? While learning, risk 0.25–0.5% of your account per trade. Shares = risk amount ÷ (entry − stop). Example: $5,000 account, 0.5% = $25 risk; stop $12 below entry → 2 shares. Round down.
  5. Already holding similar stocks? Five small tech trades are one big tech bet. Could you handle all of them gapping down together overnight?

What it can't do

  • Predict. A score of 85 means "matches the rules well today", not "85% chance it goes up".
  • Read the news. Check it yourself before buying.
  • Prove itself yet. New and untested. A second opinion, not an expert. Consider paper trading first.

    Rules v2.1: how labels, flags and the score are computed
    Benchmark each stock is compared with one benchmark: SMH for semiconductor names, QQQ for everything else. RS vs bench is the stock's 20-day return minus the benchmark's, in percentage points. Inside the score it is divided by the stock's ATR % so that +5 pts on a calm stock counts for more than +5 pts on a wild one.
    Prior 20-day high/low use sessions t−20 to t−1 and exclude today, so today's bar cannot be its own breakout level.
    Setup labels (one per stock). Breakout confirmed: close above the prior 20-day high, price above SMA20 above SMA50, SMA20 rising. Breakout watch: same trend, close within 1 ATR below the prior high and in the top 15% of the prior range. Pullback watch: SMA20 above SMA50, SMA50 rising, price above SMA50, price within −1.5 to +0.5 ATR of SMA20, RSI 40–60, RS vs bench no worse than −1 ATR. Downtrend: price below SMA20 below SMA50. Otherwise No setup.
    Flags (can coexist with any label). Extended: more than 2.5 ATR above SMA20 or RSI above 75. Heavy selling: volume 1.5× average with a close in the bottom 40% of the day's range and below the prior close. Earnings ≤ 7 days. Incomplete: benchmark data missing, score withheld.
    Breakout alignment (0–100), five buckets. Trend 25: price above SMA20 (8), SMA20 above SMA50 (8), SMA20 rising (5), SMA50 rising (4). Relative strength 25: RS vs bench in ATR units, full 15 at 2.5 ATR; benchmark regime 10: benchmark above its SMA50 (6) and that SMA50 rising (4). Setup 20: position in the prior range from 60% to 100% (0→10), within 1 ATR of the prior high (5), closed above it (5). Volume 15: relative volume scaled 0.8×→2.0×, awarded only if the close is in the upper half of the day's range and above the prior close; otherwise 0. Momentum 15: RSI ramps 40→55 (0→8), flat 55–68, ramps 68→78 (8→0); last 5 days positive (4); last 5 days stronger than the 5 before and positive (3).
    75+ strong, 55–74 moderate, 35–54 mixed, under 35 weak. Not a probability. An 80 means the stock matches the momentum-breakout rules well today. The score is shown next to pullback labels for context only; a pullback score is a later addition.
    ATR % 14-day Wilder ATR as a share of price. RSI 14-day Wilder. Avg $vol mean of close × volume over the last 20 completed sessions. Vol vs avg latest session against the 20 before it; understated while the market is open.
    Snapshots every run is saved in this browser with the rules version, source (live, live-intraday, demo), setup, flags and score so results can be checked later. Copy as CSV to keep it elsewhere.
