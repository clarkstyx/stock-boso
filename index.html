How the columns and setup labels are computed
Setup labels are rule matches, not predictions. Breakout candidate: price above SMA20 above SMA50, SMA20 rising, price in the top 15% of its 20-day range and within 1 ATR of the 20-day high. Pullback watch: SMA20 above SMA50, price above SMA50, price within −1.5 to +0.5 ATR of SMA20, RSI between 40 and 60. Extended: price more than 2.5 ATR above SMA20, or RSI above 75. Downtrend: price below SMA20 below SMA50. Otherwise no setup.
Breakout alignment (0–100) how many of the momentum-breakout strategy's conditions are met, grouped into five buckets so correlated signals don't double count: Trend 25 (price vs SMA20/SMA50, SMA20 slope), Relative strength 25 (RS vs QQQ and SMH, full marks at +5 pts), Setup 20 (range position, within 1 ATR of the 20-day high, not extended), Volume 15 (relative volume, halved on a down day), Momentum 15 (RSI zone, 5-day return, acceleration). 75+ strong, 55–74 moderate, 35–54 mixed, under 35 weak. It is not a probability. A 78 means the stock matches the strategy's rules well today, not that it has a 78% chance of rising. The earnings badge is deliberately kept outside the score.
RS vs QQQ / SMH 20-day return of the stock minus the 20-day return of the benchmark, in percentage points. Positive means it is leading the index, not just riding it.
ATR % 14-day Wilder ATR (includes overnight gaps) as a share of price. RSI 14-day Wilder RSI, matching TradingView. Over 70 is not an automatic sell; in a strong trend it can stay high.
Avg $vol mean of close × volume over the last 20 completed sessions. Vol vs avg latest session volume against the 20 sessions before it (the latest is excluded from the baseline). During market hours the latest bar is partial, so this understates activity until the close.
20d range uses intraday highs and lows: 0 = at the low, 100 = at the high.
